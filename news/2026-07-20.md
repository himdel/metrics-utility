## Inconsistencies

- The Segment field rename (`collection_name` to `collection`, `module_name` to `module`) cascaded across three PRs in two repos within days (ansible/metrics-utility#484, ansible/metrics-utility#485, ansible/metrics-service#350). Root cause was Segment silently dropping properties whose key contains "name" -- worth documenting in Segment integration guides.
- ansible/awx#16552 changes `skip_tags` from CharField(1024) to TextField on `UnifiedJobTemplate`/`UnifiedJob` -- these are tables our collectors read; the type change shouldn't break existing queries but may affect any code that assumes a max length.
